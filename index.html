How it works

A personalized learning journey, run every week.

Not a plan written once and filed away. The same five steps, repeated, so the plan keeps up with your child.

Every weekthe loop repeats

01Understand

We start with a conversation — with you and with your child — to find where they actually are, topic by topic, not just subject by subject.

02Plan

That becomes your child's personalized learning journey: which gaps to close first, and in what order.

03Teach, in five

Live online classes with one tutor and five students, with doubt support so a question never waits for the next class.

04Practise and test

Homework after class, and a weekly test that shows whether the idea has actually landed. Sessions are recorded so your child can go back over them.

05Report

A weekly progress note on WhatsApp — what was covered, how the test went, and what comes next. Then the plan is adjusted, and the loop starts again.

Questions

Fair questions.

My child is weak in maths. Is this for them?
Very likely — but “weak in maths” is usually a gap in one or two topics rather than the whole subject. Finding which ones is the first thing we do.
Is it really five students, or five on average?
Five. If a batch would need to take a sixth child, it becomes a new batch instead.
What does the weekly progress note include?
What was covered, the weekly test result and how it compares with the week before, homework, any doubts raised, and what the tutor is focusing on next. It comes on WhatsApp.
Are classes recorded?
Yes. Sessions are recorded so your child can go back over them when revising.
What are the fees?
We share the full fee on WhatsApp before the trial. It depends on the grade and how many subjects you want, and there is no long contract to sign.
Will the timing clash with school?
Classes are in the evening, Saudi time, after school hours.
